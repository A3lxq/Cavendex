### Alternate one-breath version (for a fast-cut 15–20s edit)

> "Your SOC gets ten thousand alerts a day — this AI reads every one. Triages it, investigates it, maps it to real MITRE ATT&CK — and stops. Because nothing executes without a human clicking approve. It's Cavendex — a free, open-source AI SOC analyst. Link's in my bio."

### Delivery notes
- Emphasize **"nothing executes without a human"** — that's the trust-building line, say it slower than everything around it.
- Emphasize **"free"** and **"open source"** on the end card — that's the payoff/CTA.
- If recording your own voice: record dry (no music), then let Instagram's trending-audio ducking handle the mix — don't try to time your voice to a beat manually.
- If using on-screen captions instead of voice: the video already has these burned in as shorter text cards — the voiceover above is the "long-form" version of the same beats.
